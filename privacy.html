Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Gym Bros is a small training app run by Ben Light. You can contact me at ben_light@me.com. I am the person responsible for your data.

What I collect

  • Your account: email address, password (stored scrambled by Supabase, I cannot read it), display name, and the fact that you confirmed you are 16 or older, with the date.
  • Your training: workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, cardio, plans, exercise settings and personal records.
  • Body and fat loss information, if you enter it: bodyweight, fat loss plans, daily logs (weight, calories, protein, carbs, fats, steps) and body measurements. This can count as health information, so you only provide it if you choose to.
  • Groups and challenges: which groups you belong to, your challenge progress (which challenges you have completed), and what you are training right now while a workout is in progress.

The app also keeps settings, a copy of your data for offline use, your Hyrox and challenge setups, and any changes not yet uploaded, on your own phone. Nothing is sent to me from there except the data above.

Who can see it

  • Only you can see your fat loss logs, body measurements and bodyweight.
  • People in your groups can see your display name, your weekly total volume and workouts, your personal records, what you are training live, and can compare their lifts with yours.
  • Everyone signed in to the app can see your display name and which challenges you have completed, if "Show my progress to everyone" is switched on. You can switch it off on the Board tab.
  • I can see, as the person running the app, a list of everyone using it with their display name, when they joined, last workout and number of workouts, challenge progress, and a breakdown of each person's workouts (plan, exercises, sets and total weight lifted). I cannot see your fat loss logs, measurements or bodyweight from that screen.

Why I use it

  • To run the app for you: your account, saving your training, groups, leaderboards and challenges. This is needed to provide the app.
  • For body and fat loss information, you give it to the app by entering it. You can delete it at any time, or delete your whole account.
  • I do not sell your data, show adverts, send marketing emails, or use analytics or tracking.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores the database, runs sign-in and sends sign-up and password-reset emails. Your data is stored in Stockholm, Sweden (Amazon Web Services region eu-north-1), inside the EU.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files, so GitHub receives your IP address when the app loads.

How long I keep it

I keep your data for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account and all your data at any time in the app: Plans tab, then "Delete account and all data". It is removed straight away. I do not keep backups of the database, so once you delete your account your data cannot be recovered.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to how it is used. You can download your workouts and fat loss logs yourself from the Plans tab, and delete everything there too. For anything else, email me. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

Gym Bros is for people aged 16 or over.

Changes

If I change this notice, the date at the top will change.
